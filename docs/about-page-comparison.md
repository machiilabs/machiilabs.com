# About pages from small Mac software companies

Pages read October 24, 2026. These are one-person and few-person Mac studios, the closest public peers to Mach II Labs.

Small Mac software companies do put a name on the About page. The company stays the subject. The name is usually one sentence, sometimes with a city.

Eight of these eight peer About pages name a person. The shortest founder line is one sentence (Red Sweater). machiilabs.com/about names nobody.

The page is about the studio. A founder is named anyway, usually with a city or a sentence about how it started. Product names and a contact address sit beside that. Many Tricks is the long version: two personal essays and a street address. Mach II Labs is the short version: the two apps, free forever, and two email addresses.

Hog Bay’s page includes a photo of Jesse Grosjean. Other pages may show portraits that this text pass did not capture.

## How they open

### Mach II Labs

“Mach II Labs makes the software on this site. Skagway and 15CE Flasher are free forever.”

Then a short description of each app, the free-forever promise, and contact@ / support@. Local draft: `/about`.

### Hog Bay Software

“Hog Bay Software is located in Bangor, Maine and is run by (me) Jesse Grosjean since 2004. I build mostly Mac software for writing and thinking.”

https://www.hogbaysoftware.com/about/

### Red Sweater

“Creatively Productive Since 1999.” Then: “Daniel Jalkut founded Red Sweater after working as a Software Engineer at Apple.”

That is the whole page, plus the company slogan.

https://redsweater.com/about/

### Noodlesoft

“We are based in New York City, unofficial capital of pasta-named software companies producing software solely for the Mac.”

Paul Kim is then listed as Chief Noodler, accountant, system administrator, and support. The joke is that one person is the whole company.

https://www.noodlesoft.com/about/

### Shirt Pocket

“A small company, making small but useful things.” The next line is “Shirt Pocket was founded by me — David Nanian — in late 2000,” followed by earlier companies and a milestone list back to 1983.

https://www.shirtpocket.com/about.php

### Running with Crayons (Alfred)

“Our love affair with Apple began in the 1980’s with our first Mac Plus.” Andrew Pepperrell and Vero Pepperrell are named as the founders of Running with Crayons Ltd in 2010. The rest is Alfred’s audience and awards.

https://www.alfredapp.com/about/

### Stairways Software

“Stairways Software was founded in April 1995 by Peter N Lewis.” It reads as company history, based in Perth, and closes with: when you contact them, you deal with someone who can handle the question personally.

https://www.stairways.com/main/about

### Many Tricks

“Who are we, anyway?” Then a full essay from Peter Maurer and a full essay from Rob Griffiths, a Beaverton mailing address, and a paragraph on why the company is named Many Tricks.

https://manytricks.com/about/

### Bombich Software

“We’re the original macOS backup people.” Under that, a staff list: Mike as founder, then Sarah, Rob, Mark, and Nancy with short role lines. First names and jobs, without an origin essay.

https://bombich.com/about
