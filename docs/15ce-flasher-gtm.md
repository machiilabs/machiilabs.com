# 15CE Flasher — go-to-market checklist

Short outreach plan for people who may want to flash an HP 15c Collector’s Edition. Pair with the on-site SEO foundation (`/flasher`, sitemap, OG card).

**Privacy:** no site analytics, tracking pixels, or app telemetry. Bug reports stay user-initiated.

## Where to post (priority order)

1. **MoHPC** ([Museum of HP Calculators forum](https://www.hpmuseum.org/forum/)) — primary audience for 15c CE firmware discussion.
2. **Reddit** — [r/calculators](https://www.reddit.com/r/calculators/); only if on-topic. Follow self-promo rules (often better as a helpful reply than a cold launch post).
3. **Existing HP 15c CE firmware threads** — reply where people struggle with Windows SAM-BA or Mac; link product + guide.
4. **Later (optional):** HP calculator Discords / Facebook groups you already belong to. Avoid cold spam in groups where you have no history.

## Message pattern

Lead with the problem, then the product, then links. Stay factual.

**Template (adapt per venue):**

> If you have an HP 15c Collector’s Edition and a Mac, flashing firmware usually means wrestling with Windows SAM-BA and the pogo cable.
>
> **15CE Flasher** is a free native macOS app from Mach II Labs that walks you through the flash step by step — no Windows SAM-BA required.
>
> Product: https://machiilabs.com/flasher  
> Short guide: https://machiilabs.com/flasher/guide
>
> Unofficial / not affiliated with HP. Use HP’s own firmware and follow their terms. Flashing can wipe memory or brick the calculator — read the on-page warnings and use DEMO mode first if you’re unsure.

## What we will not do

- No Google Analytics, tracking pixels, or app phone-home
- No SEO spam or doorway pages
- No bought backlinks
- No “affiliate” or paid placement for this free tool (unless you consciously decide otherwise later)

## After each post

- Watch for support questions → point to `/flasher/guide` and `support@machiilabs.com`
- Note recurring friction for a future FAQ on `/flasher` or the guide (separate task)
- After deploy, submit `https://machiilabs.com/sitemap.xml` once in Google Search Console
